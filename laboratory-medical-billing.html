Complete RCM Services

Our Outsourced Laboratory Medical Billing Services

Overall revenue cycle management spans each step of the process, from ordering a test to final payment collection.

01

Insurance Verification

Before the test, we validate coverage, benefits, and payer-specific mandates to minimize front-end denials associated with eligibility and coverage gaps.

02

Laboratory Coding (CPT, ICD-10, HCPCS)

Certified coders code CPT, ICD-10, and HCPCS codes clinically, molecularly, toxicologically, and pathologically with clean medical necessity documentation.

03

Claim Preparation and Submission

Before submission, all claims are scrubbed against payer edits to increase first-pass acceptance and measure avoidable rejections.

04

Prior Authorization Management

We secure and track prior authorizations for advanced and high-cost tests so reimbursable services aren't denied for missing approvals.

05

Payment Posting and Reconciliation

Payments and adjustments are posted accurately and reconciled against expected reimbursements to surface underpayments quickly.

06

Accounts Receivable (AR) Follow-Ups

We work aging claims systematically by payer and dollar value to recover revenue and drive down days in AR.

07

Denial Management and Appeals

Denials are root-caused, corrected, and appealed, and the underlying pattern is fixed so the same denial doesn't recur.

08

Patient Billing and Statements

Clear patient statements and responsive support improve the collection of patient-responsibility balances.

09

Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) Reporting

Transparent reporting on clean claim rate, denial trends, AR aging, and collections gives you full visibility into financial performance.
